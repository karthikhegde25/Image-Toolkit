Compress

Set a quality number, or type the exact file size you want in KB or MB. Output is JPG or WEBP, since PNG can't be quality-compressed this way.

1 = smallest file100 = highest quality

The tool finds the highest quality that fits your size, and shrinks the image only if it has to. With JPG and the option above, the file is then filled to the exact byte count. 1 KB = 1024 bytes.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Image Toolkit is free and open source. It is built to respect your privacy by doing all of its work on your own device.

Your images stay with you

Every tool runs inside your browser. The images you pick are never uploaded, sent to a server, or stored by us. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking built into this app.

Third-party services

The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser may share basic request information, such as your IP address, with Google when it does. If you host your own copy, you can remove that link and the app will fall back to your system fonts.

Open source

The full source code is public under the MIT License, so you can read exactly what the app does or run it yourself. Find it on GitHub.

Questions

Open an issue on the GitHub repository and we will get back to you.
